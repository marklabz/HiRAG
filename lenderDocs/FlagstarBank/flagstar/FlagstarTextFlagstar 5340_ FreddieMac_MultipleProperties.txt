PROGRAM SUMMARY 
Fixed and adjustable-rate, fully amortizing, conventional conforming and high balance first lien mortgage loan 
program specifically designed for borrowers with 7 to 10 financed properties.

UNDERWRITING 
All loans must meet the parameters as set out in Flagstar’s Conventional Underwriting Guidelines. 
•  Borrowers own between 7-10 financed properties. (This applies to the total number of properties 
financed, not just the number of mortgages sold to Freddie Mac). 
•   For Flagstar policies on Multiple Loans to the Same Borrower, see Conventional Underwriting 
Guidelines. 
 
RESERVES  
Minimum reserves of eight months of the monthly payment on each additional second home and/or 1- to 4-unit 
investment property that is financed and on which the Borrower is obligated.  The monthly payment amount is 
the sum of the following monthly charges: 
•  Principal and interest payments on the Mortgage 
•  Property hazard insurance premiums 
•  Real estate taxes 
•  When applicable: 
  Mortgage insurance premiums 
o
  Leasehold payments 
o
  Homeowners association dues (excluding unit utility charges) 
o
  Payments on secondary financing 
o
•  LPA may require greater reserves based on the borrowers credit profile
