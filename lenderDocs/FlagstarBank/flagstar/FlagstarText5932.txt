Community Seconds Programs 
A Community Seconds transaction is financing for structuring affordable housing. Partnerships between lenders, non-profit organizations (other than credit unions), government entities and employers to aid 
qualified home buyers. Community Seconds may not be eligible behind a first lien with Mortgage Insurance. Refer to the Mortgage Insurance guidelines for additional information. 
 
Program must be from one of the following: Government Agency, verifiable nonprofit agency (501(c)3 letter required), or employer. Funds may only be used for down-payment and closing costs unless otherwise 
noted. No cash back to the borrower is allowed. All documents must be received at one time; otherwise we will not be able to complete our review. Programs are eligible for 12 months at which time updated 
documents must be submitted. All programs are reviewed for compliance only. Flagstar Bank does not endorse, fund or underwrite any grant programs. Any lender participation required by the program would 
require the broker/correspondent’s approval for participation. Flagstar Bank's lender approval would extend to Home Lending Branches only and would not include third party originations. 
 
Unless otherwise noted in the comment section below, a Note and Mortgage/Deed of Trust will be filed as the security instruments. A government agency does not require the Community Second to be on a 
separate Closing Disclosure. However, if a Non-Profit agency charges anything other than credit counseling and recording fees, a separate Closing Disclosure is required. A copy of the lien documents and 
Closing Disclosure for the Community Second are required for the secondary lien in the closing package submitted to Flagstar.   
 
Refer to Product Guidelines, Underwriting Guidelines and MI company requirements for maximum CLTV limits. The minimum down payment requirements of the applicable first mortgage product must be met. 
Declining market restrictions do apply to LTV and CLTV. 
 
Refer to the Down Payment Assistance Submission Checklist, Doc. #3284 and Guidelines for Down Payment Assistance Programs, Doc. #5936 for additional details. Submit all required documentation via email 
to product.development@flagstar.com or fax to (888) 633-4101.  Refer to Hardest Hit Funds, Doc. #5937 for information regarding approved programs. 
 
For Refinances with existing Community Seconds the following is required for review:  copy of the Original Note and the Subordination Agreement.  Please include the Borrower Name and Loan Number with your 
request.
