DEROGATORY CREDIT 
Significant derogatory credit will follow DU findings with the exception of Chapter 13 bankruptcies and loan 
modifications: 
•  Chapter 13 bankruptcies discharged or dismissed will require 4-year seasoning from the discharge 
or dismissal date 
•  Loan modifications will require 7-year seasoning from the modification date 
•  Reduced seasoning due to extenuating circumstances is not permitted 
 
ESCROW HOLDBACKS 
Escrow holdbacks are allowed on purchase transactions of new construction properties only.  The builder must 
be the seller. 
 
Escrows are allowed if the incomplete items do not affect the marketability, safety, livability, soundness, or 
structural integrity of the property. The appraiser must provide the "as completed" value as the appraised 
value.

Postponed improvements can be escrowed if: 
•  The items are part of the builder’s sales contract (third-party contracts are not permissible);  
•  The items are postponed for a valid reason, such as inclement weather or a shortage of building 
materials; 
•  The establishment of an escrow does not impair or adversely affect the mortgage or title insurance 
during the completion period; 
•  The items do not affect the ability to obtain a certificate of occupancy. 
 
AMOUNT AND LENGTH OF ESCROW 
•  An escrow account equal to 120% of the estimated costs for completing the postponed 
improvements and/or repairs must be withheld from the Loan proceeds. However, if the contractor 
or builder can guarantee a fixed-price contract for the improvements and/or repairs it is acceptable 
to use the contract price for the escrow amount. 
•  The cost to complete the postponed improvements and/or repairs must not be greater than 10% of 
the “as completed” appraised value. 
•  Incomplete items must be completed within 180 days of the date printed on the Note. 
 
DOCUMENTATION REQUIREMENTS 
All of the following documents are required when an escrow account is set up: 
•  The appraiser must provide a list of the incomplete items; the appraiser or a disinterested (but 
relevant) party may provide a cost to complete the incomplete items. 
  An example of a disinterested (but relevant) party is a contractor/painter who provides an 
o
estimate to paint interior walls. A relevant party includes, but is not limited to, a representative of 
a home improvement store or an independent contractor that performs the services needed to 
complete the improvements. 
•  Escrow Holdback Agreement, Doc. #3656 must be fully completed and executed. 
•  A clear final inspection to be obtained at completion of postponed improvements and/or repairs 
within the specified time frame. 
•  Final title report that does not show any outstanding mechanic's liens, take any exceptions to the 
postponed improvements or the escrow agreement. If the final title report is issued before the  
completion of the improvements, an endorsement to the title policy that ensures the priority of the 
Mortgage lien must be obtained. 
 
Refer to the Flagstar Escrow holdback Procedures, Doc. #4634 for additional closing and funding 
requirements.
