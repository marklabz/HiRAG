PROGRAM SUMMARY 
This is an intermediate term adjustable-rate mortgage loan program with an initial fixed rate period of 5, 7 or 10 
years depending on the product selected. Upon the expiration of the fixed-rate term the program allows for 
increases or decreases to the interest rate every six months.
