VA Interest Rate Reduction Refinance Loan (IRRRL) 
 
PROGRAM SUMMARY 
An IRRRL is a new VA-guaranteed loan made to refinance an existing VA-guaranteed loan, generally at a 
lower interest rate than the existing VA loan, and with lower principal and interest payments than the existing 
VA loan. 
 
PRODUCTS OFFERED 
FIXED-RATE MORTGAGES 
Traditional and jumbo loan amounts are separated into distinct products: 
•  10-year 
•  15-year 
•  20-year 
•  25-year 
•  30-year 
 
ADJUSTABLE-RATE MORTGAGES 
Traditional and jumbo loan amounts are combined into the same product. Manufactured homes are not 
eligible for the ARM product.  Fixed-rate products only.

TEMPORARY BUYDOWNS

CREDIT ALERT INTERACTIVE VOICE RESPONSE SYSTEM (CAIVRS) 
All borrowers must be screened using HUD’s Credit Alert Interactive Voice Response System (CAIVRS). 
Generally, any lien must be paid-off or existing delinquent account brought current. Collect each applicant’s 
Social Security Number prior to accessing CAIVRS. 
 
APPROVED FHA LENDERS 
If your company is FHA Approved and you have access to the FHA Connection, obtain CAIVRS there. 
 
NON-FHA APPROVED LENDERS 
Non-FHA participating lenders who are currently VA Approved with a VA Agent Identification Number may 
apply for access from HUD’s CAIVRS FAQ page. If your company has not been issued a VA Agent 
Identification Number, complete the VA CAIVRS Authorization Form, Doc. #9734 to order CAIVRS. 
 
DISCOUNT POINTS 
A lender may charge reasonable discount points. 
UNDERWRITING 
•  1 and 4-unit transactions require a minimum credit score of:  
  580 for loan amounts up to the conforming 1-unit loan limit. 
o
  600 for loan amounts up to $4,000,000. 
o
•  Non-traditional, alternative credit and no credit score loans are eligible and will follow the VA Lender’s 
Handbook – VA Pamphlet 26-7. A credit score of 999 should be entered for those borrowers. 
•  To determine credit scores, a tri-merge credit report is required for all IRRRLs. If the mortgage history is 
not included on the credit report, a supplemental credit report for the mortgage history must be 
provided. 
•  An IRRRL made to refinance a VA loan over 30 days past due may not be processed and closed under 
the automatic procedure. Loan must be sent directly to VA Regional Office for prior approval and will 
require additional documentation.   
•  Manufactured homes are ONLY permitted where the existing loan is serviced by Flagstar Bank (a.k.a., 
Flagstar-to-Flagstar refinance). No other manufactured home transactions are permitted. 
 
For underwriting questions and support, email Government Underwriting Support or call (866) 945-9872.

VA ALLOWABLE FEES AND CHARGES 
Refer to Chapter 8 of VA Pamphlet 26-7 for closing costs that are allowed by VA to be paid by the borrower 
and those that must be paid by the seller or lender. For information regarding Flagstar’s administrative fee, 
please refer to Comprehensive List of Fees – Broker, Doc. #4618 or Comprehensive List of Fees – 
Correspondent, Doc. #4619.  
 
VA FUNDING FEE 
VA collects a funding fee on each loan to compensate for any losses or defaults unless the veteran is exempt.  
The funding fee is one-half percent (0.5%) for IRRRL.
