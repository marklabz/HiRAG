PROGRAM SUMMARY 
The Veterans Administration loan program enables veterans and active duty personnel to purchase or 
refinance a home with favorable loan terms. VA offers fixed and adjustable-rate mortgages that can 
accommodate no down-payment on many purchase transactions. Refer to the VA Interest Rate Reduction 
Refinance Loan (IRRRL), Doc. #5821 for borrowers who would like to refinance an existing VA loan into a 
fixed-rate loan, a lower interest rate or shorter loan term without taking additional equity out of the property. For 
all required guidelines and updates refer to the manuals through the VA website.

BORROWERS WITH FULL ENTITLEMENT 
Refer to VA Circular 26-19-23 and Circular 26-19-30 for specific requirements. Essentially, for loans 
greater than $144,000, the maximum guaranty amount as of January 1, 2020 will be 25% of the requested 
loan amount without regard to the Freddie Mac conforming loan limit (CLL). 
 
BORROWERS WITHOUT FULL ENTITLEMENT 
Refer to VA Circular 26-19-23 and Circular 26-19-30 for specific requirements. Essentially, for loans 
greater than $144,000, the maximum guaranty amount will be the lesser of: 1) 25% of the requested loan 
amount, or 2) 25% of the Freddie Mac one-unit CLL minus the amount of any unrestored entitlement 
previously used by the veteran. Loan amounts requested that do not include a 25% VA guaranty will 
require additional down payment or equity from the borrower, and thus will not be eligible for maximum 
financing. Refer to the VA Loan Limits webpage for annual county-specific guaranty limits.

SELLER CONTRIBUTIONS 
All closing costs and discount points may be paid by the seller, regardless of the amount. All other seller 
contributions are limited to 4% of the lesser of the purchase price or appraised value.

CREDIT ALERT INTERACTIVE VOICE RESPONSE SYSTEM (CAIVRS) 
All borrowers must be screened using HUD’s Credit Alert Interactive Voice Response System (CAIVRS). 
Generally, any lien must be paid-off or existing delinquent account brought current.

UNDERWRITING 
•  All loan applications will be underwritten in accordance with VA and Flagstar current guidelines. Refer 
to Flagstar’s VA Underwriting Guidelines. 
•  Non-traditional, alternative credit and no credit score loans are eligible and will follow the VA Lender’s 
Handbook – VA Pamphlet 26-7. A credit score of 999 should be entered for those borrowers. 
•  Refinance transactions require an existing lien. If no lien exists, VA financing is not permitted. 
•  The note date for all refinance loans must be on after the later of: 
  The date that is 210 days after the date on which the first monthly payment was made on the 
o
mortgage being refinanced, and 
  The date on which six full monthly payments have been made on the mortgage being refinanced.

care, etc.) after deducting all debts and obligations, including monthly federal, state, and local income taxes, 
Social Security and Medicare taxes, and monthly housing expenses. The following tables display the minimum

VA ALLOWABLE FEES AND CHARGES 
Refer to Chapter 8 of VA Pamphlet 26-7 for closing costs that are allowed by VA to be paid by the borrower 
and those that must be paid by the seller or lender. For information regarding Flagstar’s administrative fee, 
please refer to Comprehensive List of Fees – Broker, Doc. #4618 or Comprehensive List of Fees – 
Correspondent, Doc. #4619.  
 
VA FUNDING FEE 
Most veterans pay a funding fee. It is a percentage of the base loan amount and may be financed or paid by 
the borrower or seller. The funding fee tables may be found on the VA website.
