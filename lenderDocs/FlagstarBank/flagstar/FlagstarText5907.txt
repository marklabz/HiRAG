Please refer to each product description for minimum and maximum loan amounts. 
 
LICENSING 
Each participating broker must be the following: 
•  Federally-chartered 
•  HUD-approved mortgagee, and/or 
•  Possess a Texas Chapter 3A license. 
 
INELIGIBLE BORROWERS 
Non-occupant co-borrowers are not eligible. All borrowers must occupy the subject property as their primary 
residence. 
 
ELIGIBLE HOMESTEADS 
Homesteads located in urban areas must be no larger than 10 acres and may consist of one or more 
contiguous lots, together with any improvements thereon. A homestead is considered to be urban if the 
property is: 
•  Located within the limits of a municipality or its extraterritorial jurisdiction or a platted subdivision; and, 
•  Served by police protection, paid or volunteer fire protection, and at least three of the following services 
provided by a municipality or under contract to a municipality: 
  Electric 
o
  Natural gas 
o
  Sewer 
o
  Storm sewer 
o
  Water 
o
 
Homesteads in rural areas shall consist of: 
•  For a family, not more than 200 acres, which may be in one or more parcels, with the improvements 
thereon; or, 
•  For a single, adult person, not otherwise entitled to a homestead, not more than 100 acres, which may 
be in one or more parcels, with the improvements thereon.

APPRAISAL 
A new appraisal must be obtained, including both interior and exterior inspections to determine current value 
on either Uniform Residential Appraisal Report (Form 1004) or the Individual Condominium Unit Appraisal 
Report (Form 1073). 
 
Refer to Conventional Underwriting Guidelines for Declining Market and Private Mortgage Insurance policies. 
 
UNDERWRITING 
All loans must meet the parameters as set out in Flagstar Bank’s Conventional Underwriting Guidelines, as 
amended. 
•  Notice Concerning Extensions of Credit Defined by Section 50(a)(6), Article XVI, Texas Constitution, 
Flagstar Doc. #3640 or VMP Form 8032(TX) will be added as a prior to close condition. Refer to the 
Cooling-off Period section for details. 
•  A Notice Concerning Refinance of Existing Home Equity loan to Non-Home Equity Loan, Doc. #3640-A 
aka The Disclosure for Refinance of Home Equity Loan to Non-Home Equity Loan. 
•  Refinances of prior Texas home equity loans must also, according to Texas law, be considered as a 
home equity loan, and subject to all of the terms and conditions of this product, even if no new equity is 
withdrawn from the property. Such loans must be underwritten, priced and closed as Texas Homestead 
50(a)(6) loans. 
•  Borrowers may only obtain one Texas home equity loan in any 12-month period. 
•  All Fixed loans must receive an Approve/Eligible response from DU or Accept from LPA and must meet 
all DU/LPA requirements. All ARM loans must receive an Approve/Eligible response from DU. ARM 
programs cannot be targeted to Freddie Mac. 
 
REFINANCING A HOME EQUITY LOAN 
•  The borrower must be provided with the Notice Concerning Refinance of Existing Home Equity loan 
to Non-Home Equity Loan Disclosure within 3 days of the application and at least 12 or more days 
before the date of refinance.   
•  When the new loan is no longer considered a TX 50(a)(6), the restrictions that apply to a TX 
50(a)(6) loans do not apply to the new transaction (e.g., Ineligible for Property Inspection Waiver).

COOLING-OFF PERIOD 
Each loan requires a cooling-off period of at least 12 days prior to closing. The cooling-off period begins from 
the application date or the date that all borrowers, owners, spouses, etc. sign the Notice Concerning 
Extensions of Credit Defined by Section 50(a)(6), Article XVI, Texas Constitution, Doc. #3640 or VMP Form 
8032(TX). 
 
TITLE INSURANCE 
A commitment of title insurance must be provided on Form T-2 and must include all standard endorsements 
plus the following: 
•  Equity Loan Mortgage Endorsement (Form T-42) 
•  Supplemental Coverage Equity Loan Mortgage Endorsement (Form T-42.1) 
 
MORTGAGE INSURANCE 
Not required 
 
FEES 
Borrowers may not be charged in excess of 2% of the loan amount in total fees. The 2% fee cap excludes fees 
related to appraisal, property survey, title insurance premiums, title examination, lender-paid fees, 
homeowner's insurance premiums, flood insurance premiums, property taxes, escrows for homeowner's 
insurance, flood insurance and property taxes, or late charges. 
 
All standard Flagstar fees will apply, including the Administrative Fee. 
 
CLOSING 
Closing docs may be ordered through Flagstar’s Web Based Closing Docs (WBCD) service available on 
Loantrac. 
 
Closings may take place at the office of the originating lender, an attorney-at-law or a title company. Power of 
attorney and mail-away signatures are not acceptable. 
No one shall be required to sign any document in which blanks are left to be filled in. At closing, each person 
must be given copies of all documents they have signed and must sign the Acknowledgement of Receipt of 
Documents and Notice of Extension of Credit – Texas, Doc. #3639 stating so and also acknowledge that they 
have received the Notice Concerning Extensions of Credit Defined by Section 50(a)(6), Article XVI, Texas 
Constitution at least 12 days prior to closing.

POST-CLOSING 
The Texas three day right of rescission and federal three day right of rescission must run after closing. The 
Texas three day right of rescission refers to calendar days, while the federal three day right of rescission refers 
to business days. Therefore, compliance with the federal rescission period satisfies the Texas rescission 
period. 
 
After the rescission period has passed and the loan has disbursed, the closing package must be sent to 
Flagstar's corporate office in Troy, Michigan along with the Addendum to Post-Closing Checklist, Doc. #3653. 
All items listed on the checklist must be included with the closing package.
